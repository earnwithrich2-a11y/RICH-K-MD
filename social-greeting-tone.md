name: Social greeting tone
description: The user's requested tone and variety for love, goodnight, and goodmorning commands.
---

`.love` should give different sweet love words when typed. `.goodnight` should give different sweet goodnight messages, and `.goodmorning` should do the same for mornings.

**Why:** The user requested varied sweet messages instead of the fixed greetings.

**How to apply:** Preserve affectionate text and message variety when changing these commands; do not revert them to one fixed reply.
